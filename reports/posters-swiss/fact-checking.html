Course experiment
UTN, winter 2025–26
Xiaochuan Li

47 people answered ten quiz questions and could check each answer twice, with ChatGPT and with Wikipedia, in an order set at random.

Bars: average answers checked, of 10. Dots: one person each. Red: ChatGPT first. Black: Wikipedia first.

Starting with ChatGPT did not stop people checking again. At the second check, many looked up almost every answer or almost none.
