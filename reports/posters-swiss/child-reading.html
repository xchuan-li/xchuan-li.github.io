Does a word help a child read a character?

Undergraduate project
Shenzhen, 2021
Xiaochuan Li

In a reading assessment, one child could read only the simplest characters on their own, but knew more of them inside words.

Does a familiar word cue the character’s sound, or is the word read as a whole?

With the Shenzhen Learning Disorders Association. The characters shown are examples, not test items.
